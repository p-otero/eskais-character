# Licences

Every model, asset and library the engine ships or downloads must allow commercial use and redistribution. Each row
was verified from its official source on the date shown. `tooling/check-licences.mjs` enforces the allow-list in CI
for every installed package and every file in `assets/ASSETS.json`.

Model-card notes that apply to the product (not licence restrictions): the cards list surveillance and identity
recognition as out of scope, and are "not intended for human life-critical decisions"; BlazeFace short range is for
faces within about 2 m, the pose model for one person within about 4 m with the head visible. The pose model's 3D
labels were made with Google's GHUM model; the card states no licence terms beyond Apache-2.0 (whether GHUM's own
terms could carry over is not documented anywhere official: UNVERIFIED, flagged for legal review if it matters).
`/latest/` model URLs are not used: for `pose_landmarker_full`, `/latest/` served different bytes from `/1/`.
